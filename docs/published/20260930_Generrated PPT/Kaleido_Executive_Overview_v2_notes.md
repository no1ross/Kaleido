# Kaleido Executive Overview v2

This revision incorporates the executive and architecture feedback from the first review.

## Narrative changes

1. Opens with the integration pain before presenting the solution.
2. Presents framework terminology alongside executive language:
   - Queryable = Business Information
   - Process = Business Actions
   - Registry = Capability Catalog
3. Reframes the shared-layer slide around the outcome: **Build once. Reuse everywhere.**
4. Keeps the immediate adoption focus on workflow/case platforms, IVR/contact center, and standards/external integrations.
5. Positions AI as another governed consumer of the same capability catalog, not as the primary product.
6. Strengthens the prior-authorization journey as the tangible proof point.
7. Closes with the executive soundbite:

> Kaleido is not another workflow engine, channel platform, or interoperability layer. It is the shared capability contract that allows all of them to work together.
